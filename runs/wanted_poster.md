# WANTED: a printing of the Declaration of Independence with these variants

Recovered from Cipher 2's known plaintext (verified key, 99.0% oracle).
Beale's physical copy of the DOI differed from the 1885 pamphlet text:

## Variant 1 — near word #155: has EXTRA word(s) 'a'
- pamphlet: …alter or to abolish it and to institute [] new government laying its foundation on such principles…
- Beale's copy reads: …it and to institute <a> new government laying its…
- search phrase: "to institute a new government"

## Variant 2 — near word #241: MISSING the word(s) 'the'
- pamphlet: …long train of abuses and usurpations pursuing invariably [the] same object evinces a design to reduce them…
- Beale's copy reads: …and usurpations pursuing invariably same object evinces a…
- search phrase: "pursuing invariably same object evinces"

## Variant 3 — near word #467: MISSING the word(s) 'repeatedly for opposing with manly firmness his invasions on the'
- pamphlet: …with his measures he has dissolved representative houses [repeatedly for opposing with manly firmness his invasions on the] rights of the people he has refused for…
- Beale's copy reads: …has dissolved representative houses rights of the people…
- search phrase: "representative houses rights of the"

## Variant 4 — near word #497: has EXTRA word(s) 'the'
- pamphlet: …such dissolutions to cause others to be elected [] whereby the legislative powers incapable of annihilation have…
- Beale's copy reads: …others to be elected <the> whereby the legislative powers…
- search phrase: "be elected the whereby the"

## Variant 5 — near word #630: MISSING the word(s) 'out'
- pamphlet: …of officers to harass our people and eat [out] their substance he has kept among us in…
- Beale's copy reads: …our people and eat their substance he has…
- search phrase: "and eat their substance he"

## Variant 6 — near word #658: MISSING the word(s) 'of'
- pamphlet: …he has affected to render the military independent [of] and superior to the civil power he has…
- Beale's copy reads: …render the military independent and superior to the…
- search phrase: "military independent and superior to"

Most diagnostic: the ten-word omission (a dropped typeset line or a
copyist's eye-skip between repeated 'he has' clauses). Any period
printing showing it is a candidate for Beale's actual source.
